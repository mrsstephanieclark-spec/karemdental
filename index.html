Meet Your Dentist

A Dentist Who
Actually Cares

Dentistry is exciting- and it is extremely exciting to Dr. Grace Karem. She grew up in Shelbyville, graduating from Shelby County High School. She moved on to the University of Kentucky for undergrad before attending the University of Louisville School of Dentistry. She then moved to Nashville for 3 years where she became comfortable working with patients of all ages, including children as young as the age of 3!

When Dr. Karem is not in the office, you can find her exploring the outdoors and hanging out with her family. She strives to be the best dentist she can be and is constantly learning and trying to stay up to date on the latest in dentistry.

PREVENTIVE & GENERAL DENTISTRY
"Keep Your Smile Healthy."

Routine Dental Exams

Regular checkups are the foundation of a healthy smile. We recommend visits every 6 months to catch small issues before they become big ones.

Dental Cleanings

Our hygienists provide thorough, comfortable cleanings that leave your teeth feeling fresh and your gums healthy.

Dental X-Rays

Digital X-rays give us a complete picture of your oral health with minimal radiation exposure.

Gum Health (Periodontal Care)

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy mouth. We treat all stages of gum disease with precision and care.

Pediatric Dentistry

We love seeing kids! Dr. Karem has experience with patients as young as 3. We make dental visits fun, not frightening.

RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY
"Restore. Rebuild. Renew."

Tooth Fillings

Tooth-colored composite fillings that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Crowns & Bridges

Strong, natural-looking restorations that protect damaged teeth and fill gaps.

Replacing Missing Teeth

Options including implants, bridges, and dentures to restore your complete smile.

Oral Surgery

Extractions and minor surgical procedures performed with care and precision in our office.

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies don't wait. We prioritize urgent care so you're never left in pain.

COSMETIC DENTISTRY
"Your Dream Smile is Closer Than You Think."

Teeth Whitening

Professional-grade whitening that's faster, safer, and more effective than over-the-counter options.

Veneers & Bonding

Transform chipped, stained, or uneven teeth with porcelain veneers or composite bonding.

Smile Design

A comprehensive approach to redesigning your entire smile for stunning, natural-looking results.

Orthodontics

Clear aligner therapy to straighten teeth discreetly at any age.

ADVANCED & SPECIALTY SERVICES
"Modern Dentistry, Advanced Solutions."

Laser Dentistry

Minimally invasive laser treatments for faster healing, less discomfort, and greater precision.

Stress-Free Dentistry

Dental anxiety is real. We offer sedation options and a calming environment so you can relax in the chair.

Night Guards & Sports Guards

Custom-fitted guards to protect your teeth from grinding and athletic impact.

AESTHETICS & INJECTABLES
"Beauty Beyond the Smile."

Botox & Injectables

Smooth fine lines and wrinkles with expertly administered Botox and dermal fillers. Dr. Karem is a trained provider through KAMAP.

Microneedling

Stimulate collagen production for firmer, more radiant skin. A non-invasive path to a refreshed complexion.

Injectable and aesthetic services are elective and are not covered under membership plans.
